## Keeping the roster current

**Proposed policy, pending maintainer approval:** maintainers must update their affiliation in this file within 30 days of an employment change. Role changes must update this roster and `OWNERS` together, and administrators must separately review actual permissions. Affiliation does not grant a company a project seat or control of project decisions.

If admitted to CNCF, maintainers must also keep the project's entry in the [CNCF maintainer records](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/project-maintainers.csv) in sync. This draft does not assert that an entry or CNCF membership already exists.
