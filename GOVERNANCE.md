# Project governance

## Project and participation

Karpenter GCP Cloud Provider provides workload-aware node provisioning for GKE Standard; see the [README](README.md) for project goals. Anyone can contribute through the process in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). All participants follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Project decisions serve users and contributors. Contributions and leadership are based on merit, not employment or sponsorship. Maintainers disclose conflicts of interest and step aside from decisions where they cannot act impartially.

## Maintainers

[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) lists project-wide maintainers and their affiliations; [OWNERS](OWNERS) records reviewers, approvers, and emeritus approvers. Reviewers review contributions; maintainers approve changes and steward project direction, releases, and security response.

## Decisions

Changes use pull requests and applicable automated checks. Maintainers may merge their own small or routine changes without another maintainer's approval. Substantial changes receive discussion and review; significant design changes follow the [proposal process](CONTRIBUTING.md#design-proposals).

Maintainers seek consensus on appointments and removals, project goals, governance changes, and requests to CNCF or for project resources. Discussions and votes may take place in issues, pull requests, Slack, or direct messages. Substantive objections are discussed; disputed changes remain open rather than being forced through. Consensus does not require explicit approval from every maintainer or a fixed voting threshold or deadline.

Record significant non-confidential decisions and their rationale in a public issue, pull request, or relevant repository document. Private conversation transcripts need not be published. Security, conduct, and sensitive personnel matters are handled privately, with a redacted outcome recorded only when safe. People involved in a complaint do not decide their own case.

## Joining and leaving

Contributors or maintainers can propose a reviewer or maintainer appointment in an issue, pull request, or directly to maintainers. Existing maintainers agree based on sustained contributions, review quality, collaboration, and willingness to take responsibility, without an employer or fixed contribution-count requirement. The candidate must accept the role.

On appointment, update `OWNERS` and the maintainer roster as applicable, explain review and release responsibilities, and have administrators grant and verify the access needed for the role.

Anyone may resign or request emeritus recognition. Remaining maintainers may agree to remove someone unable to fulfil their responsibilities or for misconduct, after offering an opportunity to respond privately where appropriate. Inactivity prompts a conversation, not automatic removal. Update role records and revoke unneeded access on departure. Emeritus recognition carries no decision-making authority or access entitlement; returning follows the normal appointment process.

## Security and communication

Maintainers coordinate security response under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). They must establish confidential conduct-reporting contacts and an independent route for reports involving those contacts.

Community channels are listed in the [README](README.md#community) and [documentation](docs/index.md#community).
