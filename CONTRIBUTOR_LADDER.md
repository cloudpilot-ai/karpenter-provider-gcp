# Contributor ladder

> **Proposed role progression, pending adoption of [governance](GOVERNANCE.md).** Existing reviewer and approver records remain in [OWNERS](OWNERS).

Everyone can contribute regardless of employer, sponsorship, or background. All roles follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

| Role                  | Responsibilities                                                                                                  | Path to the role                                                                                                          |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Contributor           | Report reproducible issues, improve documentation, propose changes, test releases, and participate constructively | Open an issue or pull request using [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); no appointment needed                             |
| Reviewer              | Review code/docs in their area, explain feedback, and help contributors meet project standards                    | Demonstrate useful contributions and reviews; accept a maintainer nomination and appointment under governance             |
| Maintainer (approver) | Review and approve changes, steward project-wide direction, releases, docs, and community responsibilities        | Demonstrate sustained technical and community stewardship, accept nomination, and receive the governance appointment vote |
| Emeritus maintainer   | Receive recognition and optionally advise without voting or access entitlement                                    | Request to step back, or agree recognition during offboarding; return through normal nomination                           |

Code, documentation, testing, reviews, and community contributions all count. There is no minimum PR count or employment qualification. Interested contributors can open a public issue describing the area they want to help maintain. Maintainers explain gaps and next steps rather than treating nomination as an unexplained invitation-only process.

For an appointment, update `OWNERS` and applicable role records together and verify access separately. For departure or removal, follow governance offboarding, including revoking unnecessary permissions. The [maintainer roster](MAINTAINERS.md) lists current recorded approvers and emeritus recognition; it is not a list of everyone who contributes to the project.
